# What my plan makes for me

One file that says what gets made from my plan, how often, and how it all
looks. Everything made for me reads this first. `/sac vision` fills it: my
look first, then this list.

## My look

My look lives in one place: `standards/brand.md`. Change it there, never in
one output. Until it is written, anything made for me is plain black on white.

## What gets made

| What | What it shows | Reads | How often | Kept at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| My dashboard | my plan on one page | `self-as-code.md`, the five folders | any time, `/sac dashboard` | `.oac/dashboard.html` |
| My weekly report | how the week went against my floor and my daily steps | `projects/weeks/`, `evidence/days/` | Friday | `evidence/reports/` |
| My project board | my projects and this week's tasks | `projects/` | any time | `.oac/board.html` |
| My future self journey | the road from today to the future me, through the leap and every step I wrote | `## main`, `## the rewrite`, `## commits` | any time | `.oac/journey.html` |

Add a row when you want something else made. Say it the same way: 'Add a
monthly money report to vision.md, then make it.' Nothing here is made until
you ask for it. This file is the list.

## The rules every output follows

- One file that opens in my browser. Nothing loaded from the web, so it works
  with no internet.
- My look, from `standards/brand.md`. My words, from `standards/voice.md`.
- The page for its kind under `standards/outputs/` says how it is checked.
- Stays in this private space. Nothing leaves it unless I send it.
- Gentle movement, and none if my computer asks for less.
- Made for me, not off the shelf. Never the look every AI tool makes: the
  purple fade, the same rounded cards, the same font on every page, code
  font for ordinary words, boxes inside boxes.
