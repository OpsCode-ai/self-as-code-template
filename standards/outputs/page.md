# A page, built for me

Reads first: `standards/brand.md`, then `standards/voice.md` for every word
on it.

- My colours, my typeface, light or dark as I chose. Nothing else.
- The words follow `outputs/email.md`'s rules: mine, short, plain.
- It works on a phone first. One column. No decoration that says nothing.
- Checked before it is shown to me: the colours on the page are the ones in
  `brand.md`, and the words pass the same two checks as an email.
