# Outputs: one standard per kind

One page per kind of thing that gets made for you: an email, a report, a
page, an app, a dashboard. Each one says what it reads first (how you sound,
what you like to look at), what it must never do, and how it is checked. Add
a kind when you need one. Nothing made for you may look or sound like a
machine made it, or like it came off the shelf.

`vision.md`, at the top of your private space, says which of these get made
and how often. Your look is picked once from three samples (`/sac vision`)
and lives in `brand.md`. Every kind reads it, so change it there, never in
one output.
