# A report, made for me

Reads first: `standards/voice.md` and `standards/brand.md`.

- The answer first, then the evidence. A number sits in a table, not a
  sentence.
- Every number says where it came from. Anything that cannot be grounded is
  marked, never guessed.
- My colours and typeface if it is a page; plain black on white if I have
  not chosen any.
- No headings that are moods, no bold for emphasis, no summary at the end
  that repeats the top.
- Checked before it is shown to me: the reading grade, the signs of machine
  writing, and that the colours used are mine.
