# An email, written for me

Reads first: `standards/voice.md`, then `standards/brand.md` if the email
carries a signature.

- It sounds like me. The three things in `voice.md` are the measure; the
  `## Never` line is the hard rule.
- Short sentences. Plain words. One ask per email. Australian English.
- No line a machine would write: no 'I hope this finds you well', no 'please
  don't hesitate', no three things for the rhythm of it, no dashes for pauses.
- Checked before it is shown to me: the reading grade and the signs of
  machine writing. Shown to me before it is sent, always.
